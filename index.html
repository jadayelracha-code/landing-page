FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is Racha Jadayel?

What does an AI automation consultant do?

An AI automation consultant finds the repetitive tasks in a business and builds AI-powered workflows that handle them. Racha starts with a human-led discovery deep dive, then uses AI agents and n8n to build the workflow, and tests it with evals where it matters.

What can AI automate in a small business?

Common examples are lead follow-up, WhatsApp bots that answer questions or take bookings, scheduled reporting, and document handling such as reading and filing invoices. A good first candidate is a task that repeats every week and follows clear rules.

What is MCP, and why connect my tools to one AI agent?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets AI assistants connect to tools and data. With MCP, one AI agent, such as Devin or Claude, can read and update Jira, Confluence, Miro, GitHub, Figma and Notion from a single plain-language request, so you switch between tabs less.

How do you make sure AI output is reliable?

Workflows are tested with evals where it matters: automated checks of the AI's output, for example for tone, facts and privacy, before it goes live. Important actions still wait for a human OK, so you stay in control.

How does the free intro call work?

It is a free 30-minute call to get to know your business and see if automation is a fit. If it is, the next step is a human-led discovery deep dive to find the tasks with the biggest payoff. You can book it at calendly.com/jadayelracha/30min-free-call.

Do you work with clients outside Portugal?

Yes. Racha is based in Lisbon and works with clients in Portugal and internationally, with a special focus on the GCC region (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman). Work is done remotely.
