FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you turn my idea into a real product?

Yes. Racha takes ideas from a rough concept to a working MVP: discovery, prototype and launch, built AI-native with tools like Claude Code, Supabase and n8n. Her own examples include The Fourth Player, a live WhatsApp matchmaking product, and Pharmaklik, which grew to 2,000+ pharmacies.

How does the free intro call work?

It is a free 30-minute call to get to know your business and see if automation is a fit. If it is, the next step is a human-led discovery deep dive to find the tasks with the biggest payoff. You can book it at calendly.com/jadayelracha/30min-free-call.

Do you work with clients outside Portugal?

Yes. Racha is based in Lisbon and works with clients in Portugal and internationally, with a special focus on the GCC region (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman). Work is done remotely.

Which tools do you work with?

n8n, Claude, Devin, Windsurf, WhatsApp APIs, Facebook Business APIs, Supabase, Google Workspace, Notion, Mixpanel, Jira, Confluence, Miro, GitHub, Canva, Figma and Maze.
